I've had my ups and downs, my fair share of bumpy roads and heavy winds. thats what made me what I am today.`Van damme`

In a warm summer evening I got fed up by my procrastination and lack of progress in life. This was a month ago when the wake up call hit me. I needed to wake up and focus on my what am I doing in my life, building my career. I began cutting all extra dopamine sources. First, erotic materials. Second, doomscrolling. Task one is done; that's why I finally found the time to make a blog and start creating rather than consuming. With task two on hand Mornings are sacred, and ordered, following a routine I like to call (Power-up). on the contrary I have the (Wind-down) routine for bedtime. Both of which are Phone free. It is unbelievable how much difference it makes by just removing these two things. Imagine what the future will be when those get ingrained and more productive habits find their way in my life.
